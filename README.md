LOS_Estimation_Under_Ideal_Conditions_using_the_method_prescribed_by_Transportation_Research_Board_for_Multilane_Highways.py:

1. This program estimates the Level of Service of a multilane highway under ideal conditions, using capacity and volume to capacity ratio tables prescribed by the Transportation Research Board.
2. The user enters the total number of lanes, peak hour volume, peak hour factor, and design speed, from which the directional service flow rate and applicable lane capacity are determined.
3. The program computes the volume to capacity ratio for the highway and compares it against the standard thresholds for each Level of Service at the given design speed.
4. The program reports the resulting Level of Service, ranging from A through F, based on where the computed ratio falls among these thresholds.

The tables mentioned in point 1 are Table1.csv containing Design Speed and Capacity Criteria and, 
                                    Table2.csv containing Level of Service and Volume to Capacity Ratio Criteria
